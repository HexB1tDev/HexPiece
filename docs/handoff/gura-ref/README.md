# Gura Gura no Mi: reference GIFs (Whitebeard and Blackbeard)

The owner, on 2026-11-22: "Now do Gura Gura, get reference gifs from white beard and black beard both", then "When you get the material
for the reference upload it to GitHub and give me a handoff and a prompt for the next ai".

Found on Tenor (searches such as whitebeard-gura-gura, whitebeard-quake, whitebeard-marineford, whitebeard-punch, whitebeard-tsunami,
whitebeard-akainu-punch, blackbeard-gura-gura, blackbeard-quake, blackbeard-vs-law, blackbeard-marineford, kurohige-gura). 56 GIFs
were downloaded (349 MB in all, too big for the repository):

- `sheets/`: every one of the 56 as a row of 6 frames, 7 GIFs a sheet (`sheet-00-06.jpg` holds clips 00 to 06, and so on). Look at
  these first.
- `clips/`: the 30 that show the fruit's powers, as small MP4s (480 px wide, 15 fps, no sound).
- The table below: every GIF, who uses the power, what it shows, which planned move it informs, and its full-size Tenor link (the
  `gif` link downloads the original).

What the references agree on (the look to copy):

- **The quake bubble**: a glowing, see-through white sphere with a bright rim round the fist (00, 01, 18, 24, 53).
- **The air cracks like glass**: a web of jagged white lines (cyan glow at the edges) hanging in the air where the fist strikes,
  radial cracks joined by broken rings (06, 15, 19, 39, 47, 48). It forms in a flash and shatters away.
- **The shockwave**: white curved shock lines and rings racing out ahead of the fist, throwing rocks and dust (02, 04, 07, 52).
- **The ground**: a crater with white shock lines thrown up, debris and a dust dome (03, 04); from above, concentric rings pressed
  into the ground with cracks running out (32, 34); a split running across the ground (28, 37, 55).
- **The tsunami**: giant walls of water rising and rolling in (29, 51).
- **Blackbeard's way**: the open hand, fingers spread and shaking with vibration lines (29, 30); diving from the sky fist first (32);
  his darkness clips (Yami: 27, 42 to 46, 54) are not the Gura fruit and are not to be used.
