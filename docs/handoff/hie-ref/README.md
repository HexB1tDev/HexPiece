The owner, 2026-11-22: "Can you do the same as we did with light and magma? Can you create the ice fruit, the Aokiji one? Design the
models, get the references, make sure this one is also equally as smooth. The ice is custom, not Minecraft ice shit. Like actually

against Garp at Hachinosu, and the rest).

last part of each row's label). The One Piece wiki refuses automated reads (a Cloudflare check): the technique names are the series'
own (Ice Age, Ice Time, Ice Time Capsule, Ice Ball, Ice Block: Partisan, Ice Block: Pheasant Beak, Ice Saber).

What they agree on (the look to copy): his ice is **cel-shaded cyan to white, faceted like cut crystal** (hard flat faces, white edge
lines, a few bright highlights), never round or soft; frozen bodies are **encased in blocks of that crystal** or **crusted with jagged
cyan crystals growing over them** (Blackbeard, Jozu); everything he freezes throws **white frost mist** that rolls along the ground;
the frozen sea and ground are **flat pale ice sheets with white crack lines and jagged ridges thrust up**; his Logia body **shatters into
glittering ice shards** and comes back together; his own body glows with **cyan spots** when he turns to ice (Hachinosu).
