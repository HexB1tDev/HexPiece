# HexPiece 2.6.2

Bigger swords, as you asked. Nothing here has been tested in game yet; the build's checks pass.

## The katanas and Gryphon are 25% bigger
- Every katana and Gryphon is drawn 1.25 times its size everywhere you see it:
  - in your hand and at your hip, in third person;
  - in first person: the hilt at your hip, the draw, the blade between cuts, the sheathing and the charge;
  - in other players' hands and on the sea fighters.
- **Yoru is unchanged** (at 2.75 blocks it is already huge). Say if you want it bigger too.
- **All the sword animations were re-solved for the bigger blades.** The hand still pulls each blade all the way out of its saya and
  slides it all the way home, and the longer blades still clear your body, arms and head in every cut.
- **Reach:** the cuts are now traced along the bigger blades, so the katanas and Gryphon reach about a third of a block further.
  Yoru's reach is unchanged.

## Please test
- Hold, draw, cut and sheathe a katana and Gryphon, in first and third person: is the new size right? Bigger or smaller?
- Watch the draw and the sheathing in third person: does the blade stay in your hand and in the saya all the way?
