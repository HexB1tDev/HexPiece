## What the owner asked during 2.1.4 (all done; quoted so nobody undoes it)
- "the sword is wayyyy too small": the katana is drawn at 1 model px = 1 game px (`KatanaRenderer.SCALE = 1/16`), the hand grips it
  2.4 px below the guard (`GRIP`).
- "whenever combat mode is activated the left hand should rest on the sword handle on a relaxed pose (when not moving)":
  `style_swordsman_stance` (StanceLayer plays it while sheathed in combat mode and still).
- "jesus wtf is that, shoving the sword in my own face?": no fist or blade ever crosses the head. `sword_anims.clearance()` lints it
  for every animation, and wind-ups go beside the head and behind it, never in front of the face.
- "the sheath never moves out of place, it can rotate, but never move. also the swings need more heft, more punch ... a better swing
  angle, also ... the entire moveset is now right handed only, main stance looking like my image" (Malenia: the katana low in the
  right hand, point to the ground ahead). The saya only rotates about its mouth (`sword_rig.saya_matrix`). There are no two-handed
  cuts: the original request's "two-handed overhead cut" became the one-handed `makko`, by the owner's later word. The drawn guard is
  `LOW_STANCE`.
- The Ryou finisher and Ctrl + M1's quick draw (quoted in `server/Finishers` and `server/SwordServer`).
- "that is not how you sheathe a katana ... it's slamming against it's side" and "why not just make the player pull the sheath
  forward a bit and make it fit". The blade is drawn and sheathed along the **noto line**: its tip at the saya's mouth (koiguchi),
  sliding along the saya's curve (sori, radius 180 px). Between draw and sheathe beats the blade is placed from the saya's frame, not
  the hand (`sword_anims` NOTO_*, `game_blade`, and the same in `client/sword/SwordLayer`). The left hand draws the saya a little
  forward to meet it (`NOTO_PULL`).

## Where everything is
- **Assets**: `build.py` packs the owner's zip (`input/Hex_Katana_Atelier_1.20.1.zip`, sha256 checked, never altered). Every OBJ
  becomes `assets/hexhaki/katana/<key>[_sheath][_mid|_far].hkm` (`tools/katana_mesh.py`, with LODs by vertex clustering, cached in
  `build/katana-cache`), plus atlases and item models (`forge:separate_transforms`: GUI = the icon, everything else = builtin/entity).
- **Pure tables** (`sword/`, tested by `SwordTests`): `Katana` (25, grade, hamon, description), `Grade`, `SwordMove` (15 moves:
  branch, duration, cancel tick, cut windows with damage/weight/knockback/stun, motion), `SwordCombo` (strings, BUFFER 4, WINDOW 12,
  branch by on-ground and speed > 0.12), `SwordRules` (defaults), `SwordAnims` (ids sent to clients: GUARD, SHEATHE, CHARGE,
  CHARGE_DRAW, RELEASE, QUICK_DRAW, QUICK_CUT, moves from 10), `BladePaths` (GENERATED by `tools/sword_anims.py`: blade base/tip
  every half tick in the player's frame, draw/sheathe ticks, the noto table).
- **Server**: `server/SwordServer` (the active katana by NBT id `HexPieceSword`, every action, sweeps, sync), `server/HakiSlashes`
  (the slash simulated on the server without an entity: hits, the budgeted vertical-plane terrain cut with BreakEvent, bedrock stop,
  mobGriefing for NPCs, drops via destroyBlock), `server/Finishers` (freeze, trace, then the original's `Gore.halve`),
  `PieceConfig` (server toml, section `swordsman`), `PieceItems` (items, creative tab "katanas").
- **Network** (protocol "4"): `C2SSword` (PRESS, CHARGE, RELEASE, CANCEL, QUICK_DRAW), `S2CSword` (entity, katana, anim, start),
  `S2CSwordFx`, `S2CSlash`, `S2CFinisher`.
- **Client** (`client/sword/`): `SwordClient` (the playerAnimator layer `hexhaki:piece_sword`, priority 920, THIRD_PERSON_MODEL first
  person with both arms), `SwordLayer` (extends PlayerItemInHandLayer so the first-person pass keeps it: the saya at the hip from the
  animation's leftItem, the blade from the arm + forearm bend + rightItem wrist, or along the noto line), `KatanaRenderer` (mesh +
  additive shine pass, LOD by distance), `KatanaItemRenderer`, `SwordFx` (trails, glints, impact sparks), `SlashRenderer`,
  `FinisherFx` (the red line round the victim and the overlay line in the victim's view). `client/StyleInput` sends M1, Ctrl + M1
  and the right button (never while a container or a usable item takes the click).
- **NPCs**: `npc/SeaRules.styles` (pirates: 2 in 5 picks are SWORDSMAN), `SeaRules.katana(rank, pick)`, `npc/SeaFighter.swordBrain`
  (the same SwordMove tables and `SwordServer.sweep`; captains' charged slash flies as a Haki slash in the neutral colour),
  `client/npc/SwordPoses` (GENERATED: the NPC arm poses per sword animation tick, approximating the player layer), `SeaFighterRenderer`
  (the katana at the hip / in hand).
- **Tools**: `tools/sword_rig.py` (the render chain reproduced: body bend, arm, forearm bend 0.25, rightItem Z-Y-X, the hip,
  `solve_blade` IK), `tools/sword_anims.py` (all 23 animations as beats, IK-solved, densified a key a tick, linted, written to
  `assets/hexhaki/player_animation/sword_*.json`; `java_paths`, `java_npc_poses`, `preview`), `tools/sword_view.py` (the contact
  sheet renderer). `python3 tools/sword_anims.py --only kesa,yoko` regenerates some (SOLVE takes minutes for all).
- Renders judged and kept: `docs/images/sword-*.png` (contact sheets: front, three-quarter and side views of every move), and
  `captains-log-swordsman.png` / `start-menu-3-style.png`.
