2.4.6: the evil Conqueror's sounds (sound events piece.conqueror.evil_voice_male/female, piece.conqueror.evil_release_male/female).
Made from the owner's own clip, EvilConqueror.mp3 (sent by the owner in session 5, kept unchanged in input/EvilConqueror.mp3), by
tools/evil_conqueror_sounds.py (ffmpeg: rubberband, bass, asubboost, acompressor, alimiter, libvorbis). The owner: "Evil gets the sound
I sent, when it's full charge (holding G) the voice line plays " SAWAGASHII" and when released the other sound plays. Bass boost it and
increase the volume, make it more female if the player is a woman, male is this but more bass boosted."
evil_voice_male.ogg: the clip's first part (0 - 3.39 s, the voice line), heavy bass and sub boost, compressed, -12 LUFS, peaks -0.7 dB.
evil_voice_female.ogg: the same part raised 7 semitones with its formants raised 17 %, a lighter bass boost, the same loudness.
evil_release_male.ogg: the clip's second part (3.58 s - end, the release), heavy bass and sub boost, -12 LUFS.
evil_release_female.ogg: the same part raised 2 semitones (formants kept), a lighter bass boost, -12 LUFS.
2.4.8 (the owner, testing 2.4.6: "The sound of the evil conquerors is not even audible, bro. I can hear it, and it's just super
glitchy."): all four remade. The 2.4.6 boost had put the 20-60 Hz band 12 dB over the mids (2.4.7's 21 dB): sub-bass that speakers do
not play, on which the limiter clamped and pumped. Now the sub under 38 Hz is cut, the bass boost sits near 90-160 Hz where speakers
play it, a little presence near 2.5-3 kHz, gentle compression and a slow limiter, no sub boost, no saturation: about -10.3 LUFS, crest
factors 9-10 dB; the 500-2000 Hz band 4-5 dB louder than 2.4.6 for the male files, 2.5 dB for the female ones; the male release's
60-150 Hz band about +7 dB over its mids (the clip: level), the male voice's +4, the female files' about +2 and 0.
2.6.0 (the owner, testing 2.5.9: "the evil voice line increased by a ton, its barely audible" and "cut the voice line (evil) so that as
soon as the voice ends, the sound ends, cause currently after the voice line theres a weird SFX"): evil_voice_male.ogg and
evil_voice_female.ogg remade by tools/evil_conqueror_sounds.py --voice: only the voice now (0 - 1.20 s of the owner's clip, the last
90 ms tapered; the rumble and the sweep after it are gone), mixed speech first for any speaker, a phone's too (under 90 Hz cut, 350 Hz
lowered, 2.5 kHz and 5 kHz raised, a firm compressor and a soft clip): about -8.6 LUFS, its 300-4000 Hz band about 4 dB louder than
2.4.8's and no longer under a rumble. The release roars are unchanged (2.4.8's files; in game 35 % quieter through their sounds.json
volume, their reach kept).
